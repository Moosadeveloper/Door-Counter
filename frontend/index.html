    Register a person

    Add 3 photos: face straight, head turned left, head turned right (about 30-45 degrees, not a full side view). Good light, no mask or sunglasses. More photos = better recognition.

    Registered

    An Unknown person alert means the face was clearly visible but matches nobody registered. Face hidden means the person came in without the camera ever seeing their face (mask, cap, turned away, covered).
